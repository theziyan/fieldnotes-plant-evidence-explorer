HOW TO READ THIS

A mention is a starting point,
not a conclusion.

Fieldnotes separates what a source reports from what later experiments may suggest. Every record keeps its source type visible and flags the gaps a researcher should check next.

Read our research principles →
01
Find the original context

See where a plant or use was reported and what kind of source it came from.

02
Compare evidence types

Ethnobotanical reports and laboratory studies answer different questions.

03
Keep uncertainty visible

Missing details and limits travel with the record, not in the fine print.

✳
Respect knowledge. Show your work.

Traditional knowledge has living communities and histories behind it. We aim to preserve context and attribution, avoid treating knowledge as ownerless, and never turn exploratory findings into treatment claims.
